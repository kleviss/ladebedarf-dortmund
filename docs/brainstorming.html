The ISME method in five steps

  1. City-wide forecast for 2030: 3,400–7,000 public charging points from the national NLL study; commuters add about 5.5 % and tourists about 14.9 %.
  2. Three user groups to BEVs: residents, commuters (142,000 × 70 % by car), tourists. The Excel runs BEV shares from 5 % to 50 % for 2030 and 2036.
  3. Seven charging use cases (the report's Sankey diagram), each with a factor of EVs per charging point. Result: about 5,500 AC and 2,100 DC/HPC points in 2030.
  4. Coverage per Unterbezirk by walking distance to existing chargers: living 225 m, work 500 m, shopping 100 m, leisure 375 m.
  5. Ranking: coverage score (1–3) × sum indicator (BEVs, residents, points of interest and one more variable, each classed 1–5). AC points are spread to Unterbezirke by residents, workplaces and points of interest, existing points subtracted: 0 to 111 points per Unterbezirk.

What we already have

The prototype in the repo: map of 170 Unterbezirke, district breakdown, scenario sliders, ranking, CSV export, shareable links. It uses a placeholder formula and Open Data only; the city's method and data replace most of its gaps.

"How might we" prompts

Pick four for the Diverge block. Grouped by what the jury asked for.

Quick analysis per subdistrict

  • make one Unterbezirk's number understandable in 10 seconds?
  • let a planner compare two Unterbezirke, or one under two scenarios?

Transparent criteria

  • show the 3,400–7,000 uncertainty without confusing anyone?
  • make the ISME Sankey explorable instead of a static picture?
  • show which numbers are city data, which are assumptions, and who can change them?

Value for planning

  • show where 100 new street-side points would cover the most residents within 225 m?
  • bring in the competition for street space: cycle paths, pavements, greenery?
  • turn the ranking into something for a council paper or operator tender?
Idea seeds, only if the room goes quiet
  • BEV-share and year slider (2030 / 2036) that reruns ISME live, matching the Excel scenarios
  • Interactive Sankey with editable shares per use case
  • Coverage map: 225 m buffers around the 912 chargers, uncovered residential areas highlighted
  • Priority matrix: coverage score × sum indicator as a scatter, Unterbezirke as dots
  • District profile card per Unterbezirk, exportable as one PDF page
  • Placement simulator: drop new charging points, watch coverage and ranking update
  • Validation layer: occupancy of existing EV bays from the city's parking sensors

Questions for the city mentors

  1. Priority grid: what does NUMPOINTS in Fläche mit hoher Priorität für LIS-Ausbau count?
  2. Districts: the statistics have 145 units, Open Data has 170 Unterbezirke. Is there an official mapping?
  3. Excel: customer parking is labelled 10.3 % but the formula uses 1.3 % (=C22*0.013). Which is right?
  4. Ranking: which four variables make up the sum indicator? The report names BEVs, POIs and residents; the second one is unreadable in our copy.
  5. Scope: 2030 (report) or 2036 (Excel)? AC only (report) or AC plus DC/HPC?
  6. Chargers: do the 912 points include lantern charging and private but publicly accessible sites?
  7. Users: who in the city would use this first, and for which decision?
